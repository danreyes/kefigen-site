Rig King · in development

What we're building

Chase the rig.
Get aboard.
Stay aboard.

A truck hauls a flatbed round the track while you and your rivals fight to drive up its narrow ramps and hold on. Every second aboard counts. Roof rockets, an oil spill and a spare tyre fired out of the boot are there to help the others lose their spot.

playrigking.com (the game's own site, live at launch)

Genre

Arcade car combat with a single moving objective. Grassroots Australian motorsport: beat-up street cars, shed-built equipment and questionable judgement.

Platforms

iPhone and iPad, Android, and Apple TV. Touch on phones, game controllers everywhere.

Couch play

On the TV, scan a QR code and your phone becomes a steering wheel with pedals. Two players share one screen.

Tracks

A country dirt speedway, a rainforest ridge road, a sea-cliff bridge, a river crossing by ferry, a road train on the highway and a sandstone gorge at dusk.

Status

Playable on iPhone through TestFlight and on Android test builds. Store release is in preparation.

The studio

How we work

Kefigen is small by design. We keep the whole game in one head, prove the risky physics first, and test on real phones and a real TV every week.

01

Physics first

The moving trailer is the hook. Boarding, fighting aboard and falling off had to be fun before a single track got dressed.

02

Built with AI agents

Unity, Blender and AI coding agents working from a written design that is the source of truth. Decisions are logged, not remembered.

03

Devices, not demos

Frame times, memory and heat are measured on mid-range Android phones, iPhones and Apple TV. Editor numbers do not count.
